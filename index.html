Biography

About

Xinyi Fu is an Associate Professor at The Future Laboratory, Tsinghua University. She received her Ph.D. in Design from the Academy of Arts & Design, Tsinghua University in 2018, and her Bachelor's degree from the Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University in 2012.

Her research focuses on multimodal sensing, computing, and interaction in living spaces. Her work emphasizes sensing and modeling human behavior and psychology through multimodal signals to build human-centric intelligent systems.

Interests

Research Directions

Human-Computer Interaction

Natural HCI, human activity recognition, smart spaces, and human-centric intelligent systems.

User Experience

Multimodal sensing and modeling of affect, behavior, and psychology in living scenarios.

Computational Aesthetics

Digital cultural heritage, VR/AR experiences, and emerging computing technologies for cultural preservation.
