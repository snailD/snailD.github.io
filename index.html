Xinyi Fu 傅欣奕

Associate Professor, The Future Laboratory, Tsinghua University

I am an Associate Professor at The Future Laboratory, Tsinghua University. My research focuses on multimodal sensing, computing, and interaction in living spaces, with an emphasis on modeling human behavior and psychology to build human-centric intelligent systems.

I received my Ph.D. in Design from the Academy of Arts & Design, Tsinghua University in 2018, and my Bachelor's degree from the Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University in 2012.
